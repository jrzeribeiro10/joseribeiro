01 — Who I am

About

Porto, Portugal UPorto — Videogames & Multimedia

I'm José Ribeiro, a young software developer with intensive experience in Unity and Unreal built over the past 3 years. I've been working on projects dealing with VR, AR, and 3D environments, always trying to put my skills into practice and be up to date with the evolution of the industry. I graduated from the University of Porto with a degree in Videogames and Multimedia Applications, which gave me good core bases in both technical and creative game development.

Apart from my academic background, I have gained very useful professional experience by working on game development projects throughout my summer holidays. I have always been in love with the creation of immersive worlds and interactive experiences that keep moving me forward to study new technologies and methodologies available in the field.

Beyond game engines, I also build mobile applications. I work with Kotlin in Android Studio to develop native Android apps, which lets me take an idea all the way from a prototype to something running on a phone — and it pairs naturally with the AR work I already do on mobile.

I am a developer who loves collaborative environments where my experience would be useful and, at the same time, learning from others. Always up for a challenge, pushing the limits of how much can be achieved in game development is something that interests me.

Exaud Meets

An internal 3D application for virtual meetings, built on a model-view-controller architecture with Photon Unity Networking (PUN 2) for real-time multi-user interaction.

  • Unity
  • PUN 2
  • Scrum

I have worked on an internal project called Exaud Meets, a 3D application meant for virtual meetings. In this project, I have performed very different tasks, from feature implementation to bug fixing. Scrum was followed for the management of the project, and version control was done on Git. I have been part of the code review process for code quality and coherence.

The project was implemented with model-view-controller architecture, using Photon Unity Networking - PUN 2 - for real-time multi-user interaction. During the whole development, my responsibilities included implementing the calendar, animations, performance optimization, enhancements in lighting, physics, and the implementation of communication. My contribution accordingly spreads over most aspects of the project; hence, I have hands-on experience with everything, starting from technical improvements up to feature development.
